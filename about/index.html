Hey, what’s good? I’m Berns.

Bernard Bannor, known as Berns, is a multidisciplinary designer and presenter working between culture, sport and community.

Scroll
01 / Design

Designer

Brand, digital and motion, from identities to title sequences.

After five and a half years as a Digital Designer in Guardian Advertising, creating co-branded content, display advertising and animation, Berns moved into product design and now works as a Product Designer at The Guardian. Through his studio, Berns Studios, he has led creative production for clients across music, hospitality, interiors, sports and the arts, from brand identities to animated title sequences for feature film.

Community
