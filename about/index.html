Hey, what’s good? I’m Berns.

Bernard Bannor, known as Berns, is a multidisciplinary designer and presenter working between culture, sport and community.

Scroll
01 / Design

Designer

Brand, digital and motion, from identities to title sequences.

After over five years in the advertising division, Berns stepped into product design and now works as a Product Designer at The Guardian. Through his studio, Berns Studios, he has led creative production for clients across music, hospitality, interiors, sports and the arts, from brand identities to animated title sequences for feature film.
